## Conclusion (operator, 2026-11-19)

**sel-v1 stays the default.** sel-v2 is not adopted. Choosing a policy is the project owner's call (PRD 16.3).

| | sel-v1 → sel-v2 |
|---|---|
| Reproduced and fixed | L06: negated words ("C#/.NET 없이", "Azure 계정 없이") were searched as if wanted → fixed by negation handling |
| | L07: retrieval miss (0 concepts) → now retrieved, but rank 1 is the wrong skill (m365-agent-evaluator over skill-creator) |
| Regressed | L04: extra concepts (랜딩, 페이지) dilute task_fit below `hold_fit` → NO_SKILL first |
| Holdout | Top-1 unchanged (0.857), precision@3 0.583 → 0.625. 7 draft tasks: no conclusion |

### Improvement hypotheses (for the next audit)
1. **Concept dilution.** `task_fit` is the matched share of *all* query concepts, so a richer analysis lowers fit. Score the matched share of the top idf concepts instead, or cap the denominator.
2. **NO_SKILL baseline is a constant** (fixed source and cost 1.0, fit 0.5). Compare NO_SKILL only through thresholds, or calibrate `no_skill_fit` from measured NO_SKILL success rates (`eval_runs`), once step 1 experiments exist.
3. **Generic vs specific skills** (L07, L13): domain-specific skills (m365-*, theme-factory) beat the generic one. Add a specificity signal (platform or product tokens absent from the query) beyond the current platform list.

These labels are draft-unreviewed, so every number above is an internal preliminary result.
